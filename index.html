BOBA!!!

  • Record Breaking: The largest bubble tea ever made was nearly 180 gallons in Taipei in 2018, created by a Japanese YouTuber.
  • It’s Not Just a Drink: Boba-themed apparel, popular music, and specialized festivals are common, reflecting a, global "boba culture".
  • Healthy Potential: While many boba drinks are high in sugar, you can customize the sweetness level, and the tea base itself is quite healthy.
